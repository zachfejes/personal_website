About

A little about me

I'm a trained electrical engineer turned software architect, working in the industry I care about most: space. I've always dreamed of becoming an astronaut, and I'm working towards it every day.

For five years I led the ground software architecture for Canadarm3, the robotic arm Canada designed for the Lunar Gateway space station. Today I'm the Ground Segment Architect at MDA Space, working across programs bound for the lunar surface, cislunar space and Earth orbit.
