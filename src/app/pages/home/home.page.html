About

A little about me

I'm a software architect in the space industry. At MDA Space I'm the Ground Segment Architect, setting the direction and design for the ground systems of several programs. For the past five years I've led the ground software architecture for Canadarm3, the robotic arm Canada is building for Gateway, the international space station planned to orbit the Moon: the software that mission control on Earth, and crew aboard the station, use to operate it.

My goal is to become an astronaut, and to help build what humanity needs to live and explore beyond Earth. I trained as an electrical engineer, moved into software because it let me experiment faster, and have co-founded three companies along the way. Outside of work I draw in ink and pencil, model in Blender, play piano, read constantly, and pick up new skills wherever I can.
