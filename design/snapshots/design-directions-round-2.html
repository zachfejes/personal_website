Direction C

Site Model

An architect’s study model on a warm desk. The hero is a kraft-brown topography with white paper blocks, italic annotations, a scale bar, a north arrow, and marching dashed paths. Ember-orange highlights pick out the important line, like in your data visualizations.

From your references: the topographic site model, the isometric recycling diagram on grainy paper, and the flowing data lines (Race to Treat Global Diseases, Air Pollution Sources).
